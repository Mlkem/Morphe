# Aeroplan cart and order review

In Morphe select **Aeroplan shopping** for Amazon Canada or **Aeroplan shopping for Walmart Canada**. In Amazon tap **Web cart review** for the cart checker, or **Activate for Amazon** to shop in the native app. In Walmart tap **Activate for Walmart**, or open **Patch Tools → Aeroplan shopping**. With Walmart’s **Patch Tools — Canada** selected, you can reopen **Aeroplan shopping** from its tools menu.

Activate before adding items. Sign in on the official eStore page, check the merchant’s current exclusions and tap **Shop & earn**. For web review, continue shopping in the same window. The helper displays the current host and when it observed the referral. A changed affiliate parameter or navigation away from the retailer prompts a tracking warning. A mobile app or another browser has a separate session.

**Walmart native shopping is the default.** Sign in and tap **Shop & earn**. Once the official referral supplies a Walmart shopping link, the helper sends that exact destination to Walmart Canada and closes. Continue shopping and checking out in the native app. The handoff does not establish that points will post. For the web cart checker and automatic order capture, turn off **Shop in the Walmart app after activation** before the referral completes. If Android cannot open Walmart, retry the same link or switch to web review.

If Walmart stalls, the banner shows the last stage (eStore offer, confirmation, redirect service, Rakuten or Walmart.ca), the time spent there and any error Android reports. The helper can follow the supplied redirect once automatically. **Continue** follows an available partner link. **Retry referral in Walmart** and **Browser** reuse the exact official link, including if Walmart itself starts loading and stalls. When no link is available, **Restart in browser** opens the official eStore for a fresh visit. A successful browser handoff closes the review window.

A Walmart publisher match means the retailer landing identified the same publisher as the official Rakuten click. A different publisher prompts a new referral. Conflicting fields leave tracking unverified. Changes to individual click IDs do not replace the publisher. Referral details stay in memory for this visit.

Product, cart and checkout pages show warnings for recognized retailer departments. Tap **Review** for the item names grouped by category. The helper checks public same-store product breadcrumbs when a cart row lacks its department. A missing row count, unloaded items, unknown category or expired offer stays unverified. When checkout hides every line, the helper labels the last cart review and asks you to verify changes in the cart. An exact category in an explicit inclusion or exclusion list on the current offer takes part in the review. Ambiguous terms require manual review. The dated category warnings cover the November 2026 offer through November 30. The official [Amazon offer](https://aeroplan.rewardops.com/en-CA/affiliate-details/amazon) and [Walmart offer](https://aeroplan.rewardops.com/en-CA/affiliate-details/walmart) govern earning.

In the Amazon web cart, each line shows whether Amazon’s visible add time comes before or after the observed eStore referral. Missing timestamps, an unrecognized publisher or an expired visit show **Timing unknown**. The 24-hour reminder asks you to start a fresh visit. The merchant’s official offer governs purchase deadlines. Closing or changing shopping windows can break the observation.

**Orders** saves references recognized on confirmation pages. You can also add a number manually, compare it with the official eStore history, mark the match yourself, or clear all records. New Amazon confirmation records include the referral observation and the last reviewed basket counts when available. Open the order and tap **Copy this order summary** to copy it. Earlier entries retain their dates and manual matches. The newest 100 references stay in the app’s private no-backup directory. A saved order or observed redirect does not establish a points award.

This shopping window does not read login fields, add its own affiliate tags, edit the cart or submit an order. The retailer and Aeroplan handle normal website traffic. No helper analytics or third-party backend receives the cart or order log. The stores have separate logs. Phone checkout and actual points posting require a member transaction.
